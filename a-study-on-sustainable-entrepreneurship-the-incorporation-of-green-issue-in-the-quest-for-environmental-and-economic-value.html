A Study on Sustainable Entrepreneurship: The Incorporation of Green Issue in the Quest for Environmental and Economic Value

Ati Agarwal
Assistant Professor, Shivajirao Kadam institute of technology, ati.agarwal07@gmail.com
ORCID-https://orcid.org/0009-0007-5205-9425
Anita Poonia
Assistant Professor, JECRC University, anita.poonia@jeereu.edu.in
DOI: - _________________________
Keywords: sustainable entrepreneurship; green practices; eco-innovation; competitive advantage; financial and environmental performance; Small and Medium-sized Enterprises; emerging markets
Abstract
This research analyzes the connection between business performance and the use of green business practices. It uses data from an earlier study to examine green practices and the profitability and competitiveness of businesses. The data set also contains information about the green business practices and the conditions of the business. The data was collected using 250 questionnaires. Each participant was asked to assess the inclusion of green practices in sustainable businesses. The respondents were given the choice to respond that they strongly agreed, agreed, were neutral, disagreed, or strongly disagreed. A total of 59.2% of respondents (strongly) agreed, 12.8% were neutral, and 28.0% (strongly) disagreed. Respondents' mean assessment was 3.52. A recalculation of the mean using the frequencies of the responses showed the mean to be 3.68. The standard deviation, reported as 25.11, is considered invalid. Therefore, it was not used. Given the findings from the earlier study and respondents' assessments of mean 3.68, it is likely that respondents believed that the inclusion of green practices in businesses was beneficial. It should be noted that the belief by participants does not indicate an improvement of businesses' bottom-line or competitive performance. Likewise, it does not indicate an improvement of a business's performance in relation to environmental sustainability. The study says that future studies should perform reliability and validity tests and use other research tools such as correlation and regression analysis and calculate effect sizes to analyze research data. Furthermore, the study says that if the data allows, researchers should use mediation and moderation analysis.
