1. Introduction

The International Journal of Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing and Cyber Security (IJAQC Online) is an international, peer-reviewed, open-access scholarly journal dedicated to publishing high-quality theoretical, empirical, and interdisciplinary research in the rapidly evolving fields of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Quantum Computing (QC), Cyber Security (CS), Data Science, Intelligent Systems, and emerging digital technologies. The journal serves as a global platform for researchers, academicians, scientists, industry professionals, policymakers, and technology innovators to disseminate original research, novel methodologies, practical applications, and technological advancements that contribute to the future of intelligent computing.

IJAQC(Online) aims to bridge the gap between academic research and industrial innovation by promoting interdisciplinary studies that integrate artificial intelligence with quantum technologies, cyber resilience, cloud computing, blockchain, the Internet of Things (IoT), robotics, digital transformation, and next-generation computing paradigms. The journal encourages collaborative research addressing real-world challenges in business, healthcare, education, finance, manufacturing, agriculture, governance, smart cities, and sustainable development.

IJAQC(Online) follows a rigorous double-blind peer-review process to ensure the highest standards of academic quality, originality, integrity, and ethical publishing. The journal adheres to internationally recognized publication ethics and promotes reproducible, transparent, and impactful research. Authors are encouraged to submit interdisciplinary contributions that advance scientific knowledge while addressing global technological, societal, environmental, and economic challenges.

The journal aspires to become a leading international forum for the exchange of cutting-edge research and innovative ideas, fostering collaboration among universities, research laboratories, industry, government organizations, and international scientific communities. Through the publication of high-quality research, IJAQC(Online) seeks to contribute to the advancement of intelligent technologies that are secure, ethical, sustainable, and beneficial to society.
