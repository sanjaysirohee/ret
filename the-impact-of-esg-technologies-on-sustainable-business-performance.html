The Impact of ESG Technologies on Sustainable Business Performance

Dr. Neha Goyal
Assistant Professor, University of Delhi, phdresearchb@gmail.com,
Tanishka Dabas
PhD Scholar, tanishkaadabas04@gmail.com
DOI:-
Keywords: ESG technologies; sustainable business performance; electric vehicles; systematic literature review; environment; social; digital transformation; corporate governance
ABSTRACT
In recent years, digitalization has been developing and there has been a strong drive towards environmental, social and governance (ESG) responsibility as well. These both things happened at the same time, for ESG accountability, businesses are using digital technologies. So the question is: How do these digital technologies translate into sustainable business performance? This study is specific to the EV industry, and investigates just this one question: how exactly can AI data systems and digital tracking tools make companies more sustainable and better-performing? Electric vehicle industry selected to have high expectations of environmental performance, tight government regulations and fast technological development. We conducted a search on Google Scholar in 2021–2026 using three Boolean strings following the guidelines of PRISMA 2020. A total of 25 studies passed the title/abstract screening and the full-text screening phase from the 113 records identified. All have been quality-appraised and analysed using a three-stage thematic synthesis and results were interpreted using four theoretical lenses (stakeholder, institutional, natural-resource based and legitimacy theory). Our research found out that ESG technologies like AI and other digital tools help the companies to monitor their environmental impact such as pollution they have caused energy they have used and the battery life. The companies can use blockchain and battery passports to trace the material and its source, making the supply chain transparent and responsible. AI helps to create better ESG reports, but it also helps companies avoid making false claims, or over-hyping their environmental measures, which is known as “greenwashing.” But all that can only be achieved if the company has the right skills, systems and strategy in place to implement ESG technologies and thereby improve innovation, business stability and also the financial results. One thing that this study does have is that it is geographically biased, as China is the leader in the EV industry worldwide, so the results may not necessarily be applied to the listed companies in all countries. The research is particularly focussed on the electric vehicle industry. Describes the importance of ESG technologies, or technologies that are used by companies to improve their sustainable performance. It also sets out an agenda for future research.
