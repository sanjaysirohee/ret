Online Consumer Engagement: Scopus-Based Systematic Review and Future Research Directions

Research scholar, nishasin2807@gmail.com

Keywords: Online consumer engagement, digital consumer engagement, systematic literature review, determinants of online consumer engagement, outcomes of online consumer engagement.
ABSTRACT
Online consumer engagement, also known as digital consumer engagement, is crucial for understanding different contexts and identifying potential outcomes for both academic and practical implications. It enriches studies related to business, management, finance, and accounting. Hence, to examine the trends, meanings, determinants, and outcomes of online consumer engagement, and to provide a conceptual framework for future research, this paper follows a systematic literature review process in line with PRISMA 2020 guidelines, based on articles published in the Scopus database over the last ten years. The findings suggest that online consumer engagement is a multidimensional construct divided into behavioural, cognitive, emotional, and social dimensions, which is influenced by content-related factors, firm and platform strategies, consumer characteristics, and social influences. Engagement outcomes are categorised from immediate behavioural responses to longer-term relational and performance-related effects. The conceptual framework prepared based on the findings will be helpful for academicians as well as businesses in understanding the factors that may influence consumer engagement in the digital landscape and the outcomes that may be derived from engaged consumers.
