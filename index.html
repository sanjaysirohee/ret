About Us

An International Platform for Quality Research and Academic Excellence

The International Journal of Retailing and Marketing (IJRM) is a peer-reviewed, open access scholarly journal dedicated to publishing high-quality research in retailing, marketing, management, commerce, business, and allied disciplines.

IJRM aims to promote academic excellence, innovation, and global knowledge sharing by providing researchers, academicians, practitioners, and policymakers with a transparent, ethical, and internationally recognized publication platform.

Why Choose Us!

Why Researchers Trust IJRM?

The International Journal of Retailing and Marketing (IJRM) is committed to promoting high-quality, ethical, and impactful research through a transparent publication process, global accessibility, and academic excellence.

Rigorous Peer Review

Every manuscript undergoes a rigorous double-blind peer review process to ensure originality, quality, fairness, and scholarly integrity.

Open Access Publishing

All published articles are freely accessible worldwide, increasing visibility, readership, citations, and global knowledge sharing.

Ethical & Quality Standards

IJRM follows internationally recognized publication ethics and best practices, promoting transparency, credibility, and responsible research publishing.
