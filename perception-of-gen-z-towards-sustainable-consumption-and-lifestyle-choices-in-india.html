Perception of Gen Z towards Sustainable Consumption and Lifestyle Choices in India

Dr. Neha Goyal
Assistant Professor, University of Delhi, phdresearchb@gmail.com
Vishakha Bharti
PhD Scholar, 42vishakhaxlc@gmail.com
DOI: - ______________________
Keywords: Gen Z Perception, Sustainable Lifestyle, Green consumption, Youth Behavior, Environmental Sustainability, India
ABSTRACT
Sustainable development is an issue of global significance, urging conscious decisions and lifestyle changes from each individual, especially from younger generations. Gen Z as one of the most active and well-informed generation plays an indispensable part in forging a sustainable future for the years to come. The present research paper endeavors to investigate Gen Z perception towards sustainable consumption and life style practice in India with an objective to explore their awareness level, attitude and behavior patterns in relation to sustainability and responsible consumption. “Primary data has been collected through a structured questionnaire of five points scale (Likert Scale)”. The survey was conducted by the investigator through Google forms, collecting response from Gen Z of various age groups. The data was analyzed in order to bring out meaningful and useful insights from the responses. It is proved by the results that the majority of Gen Z respondents hold positive attitude toward living a sustainable life, as well as have the knowledge of the sustainable development. Many people from young generations stated willingness for reduction of waste and usage of green products, and supportive towards green practice in their life. Internet platforms, educational institutes and government efforts can contribute a lot for promoting awareness as well as behavior regarding the sustainable development. In conclusion, Gen Z can do a lot for the sustainability development through lifestyle modification and consumer practice; moreover, the policy makers, educationists and researchers need to look forward to increase the awareness programs for young people and its proper implementation with efficient government policies and technology usage to enhance sustainable development for the nation and nation at global level.
