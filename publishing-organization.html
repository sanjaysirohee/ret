Her training expertise includes Outcome-Based Education (OBE), NEP 2020, research methodology, academic writing, innovation and entrepreneurship, digital learning, leadership, communication skills, and professional ethics. Her programmes aim to improve teaching effectiveness, research capabilities, and the professional skills of educators, researchers, and students.

As the Managing Publishing Organization of Radiant Education Trust, she is committed to promoting quality education, faculty empowerment, and capacity building through academic and professional development initiatives.

Prof. (Dr.) S. L. Gupta

Publishing Organization, Radiant Education Trust

Prof. (Dr.) S. L. Gupta is a distinguished academician, trainer, researcher, and educational administrator with over 35 years of experience in higher education across India and the Sultanate of Oman. His expertise includes teaching, faculty development, curriculum design, research supervision, academic leadership, institutional development, quality assurance, accreditation, and outcome-based education. He has served as Professor, Director, Dean, Academic Coordinator, and Research Coordinator at institutions including Birla Institute of Technology, India, and Waljat College of Applied Sciences, Muscat.

He has designed and conducted Faculty Development Programmes (FDPs), Management Development Programmes (MDPs), Research Methodology Workshops, Entrepreneurship Development Programmes (EDPs), quality assurance and accreditation programmes, leadership development programmes, and skill development workshops. His training areas include research methodology, case writing, academic writing and publishing, Outcome-Based Education (OBE), NEP 2020, innovation and entrepreneurship, digital learning, artificial intelligence in education, leadership, communication skills, and professional ethics.

An accomplished researcher and author, Prof. Gupta has supervised doctoral scholars, completed sponsored research projects, published in national and international journals, authored textbooks, and organized academic conferences, seminars, and workshops. As a Publishing Organization of Radiant Education Trust, he continues to support quality education, faculty empowerment, research excellence, professional development, and capacity building.
