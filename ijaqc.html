International Journal of Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing & Cyber Security (IJAQC)

The International Journal of Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing & Cyber Security (IJAQC) is an international, peer-reviewed, open-access scholarly journal dedicated to publishing high-quality, original, and innovative research in the fields of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Quantum Computing, Cyber Security, Computer Science, Information Technology, Data Science, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Blockchain, Internet of Things (IoT), Cloud Computing, Robotics, Natural Language Processing (NLP), Computer Vision, and other emerging technologies.

REVIEW PROCESS OF (IJAQC)

The method used to determine the content of a manuscript before it is published online is the peer review process. Independent professionals/experts/researchers in the related research sector are expected to evaluate the uniqueness, validity and relevance of the submitted manuscripts to assist editors in deciding whether a manuscript should be published in their journal

This process of peer review helps validate the research work, create a method by which it can be assessed and expand the possibilities of networking within research communities. Despite criticism, peer review is still the most generally accepted means of validating science

Only papers submitted with bibliographic reasoning that follow good scientific criteria, explanations, records and proof of their work (e.g. understanding and building on other work in the field, relying on rational reasoning and well-designed experiments, supporting statements with evidence, etc.) are approved for publication in the Journal.

WE FOLLOW DOUBLE-BLIND PEER REVIEW PROCESS

We usually follow the method of double-blind peer review, in which both the authors and the editors who will review the submitted papers and authorize them for publication are unaware of the identity of each other.

The editor of the journal assigns the papers obtained from the researchers to the reviewers in this process, in which the reviewers are initially expected to check the scope of the manuscript, whether it suits the journal or not, to provide their comments or any suggestions/edits in the paper (if necessary) to accept the manuscript for publication in the journal.

This constitutes the basis for determining if the work should be approved, revised as acceptable, or rejected. Submissions with significant shortcomings would be rejected, but after they have been thoroughly revised, they can be re-submitted.

This does not necessarily mean it is of low quality if a job is refused. A paper can also be rejected because it does not fall within the specialis ation field of the journal or because it does not meet the high expectations of innovation and originality that the journal in question demands.

If the reviewer recommends only minor edits, the journal will publish the article, but the author is asked to make certain corrections before that.

Article Correction Policy

International Journal of Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing & Cyber Security (IJAQC)

The International Journal of Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing & Cyber Security (IJAQC) is committed to maintaining the accuracy, integrity, transparency, and reliability of the scholarly record. Despite rigorous editorial screening and peer review, errors may occasionally be identified after publication. When such errors are brought to the attention of the Editorial Office, the journal will take appropriate corrective action in a fair, transparent, and timely manner.

Corrections are made only when the identified error materially affects the accuracy, interpretation, reproducibility, scientific validity, or integrity of the published work. Minor typographical or formatting errors that do not influence the scientific content generally do not require formal correction.

1. Purpose of Article Corrections

The purpose of the Article Correction Policy is to:

Preserve the integrity and credibility of the scholarly record.

Correct significant scientific, technical, or editorial errors.

Ensure transparency in scholarly communication.

Maintain the confidence of authors, reviewers, readers, and indexing agencies.

Protect the reputation of the journal and its published research.

.Types of Post-Publication Corrections

A. Erratum

An Erratum is issued when a significant error has been introduced by the journal during the editorial or production process.

Examples include:

Incorrect author names or affiliations.

Errors in the article title or abstract.

Incorrect figures, tables, equations, or algorithms introduced during production.

Missing acknowledgements or funding information due to editorial oversight.

Production or formatting errors affecting interpretation of the published work.

All errata will be published promptly and permanently linked to the original article.

B. Corrigendum

A Corrigendum is issued when authors identify a significant error in their published article that affects the accuracy or clarity of the research but does not invalidate its overall findings.

Examples include:

Errors in datasets or numerical values.

Incorrect equations, algorithms, or computational models.

Mistakes in references or citations.

Errors in software implementation or methodological descriptions.

Incorrect figures, graphs, tables, or supplementary materials prepared by the authors.

All authors must approve the corrigendum before publication.

C. Addendum

An Addendum provides additional peer-reviewed information that enhances or clarifies a published article without altering its original conclusions.

Examples include:

Additional experimental or computational results.

Clarification requested by readers or reviewers.

Supplementary datasets or software documentation.

Additional technical explanations omitted unintentionally during submission.

Addenda are subject to editorial evaluation and, where appropriate, independent peer review.

D. Retraction

A Retraction is issued when a published article is found to contain serious ethical, scientific, or technical problems that render its findings unreliable.

Retraction may occur in cases involving:

Plagiarism.

Data fabrication or falsification.

Image manipulation.

Fraudulent or manipulated research results.

Duplicate or redundant publication.

Plagiarism Policy

International Journal of Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing & Cyber Security (IJAQC)

The International Journal of Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing & Cyber Security (IJAQC) is committed to maintaining the highest standards of academic integrity, originality, ethical publishing, and responsible research. Plagiarism in any form is considered a serious violation of publication ethics and is strictly prohibited.

All manuscripts submitted to IJAQC must represent the original work of the author(s). Authors are responsible for ensuring that their submissions are free from plagiarism, duplicate publication, data fabrication, data falsification, image manipulation, unauthorized use of Artificial Intelligence (AI)-generated content, and other forms of academic misconduct.

The journal screens every submitted manuscript using internationally recognized plagiarism detection software such as Crossref Similarity Check (iThenticate) or equivalent tools before initiating the peer-review process. Manuscripts found to contain significant plagiarism or unethical overlap may be rejected immediately or subjected to further investigation in accordance with this policy.

1. Definition of Plagiarism

Plagiarism is the presentation of another person's ideas, words, research findings, algorithms, software, datasets, figures, tables, images, code, or intellectual property as one's own without proper acknowledgment or permission.

Plagiarism includes, but is not limited to:

Copying text without appropriate citation.

Paraphrasing another author's work without proper acknowledgment.

Using datasets, algorithms, software code, figures, tables, or images without permission or attribution.

Submitting another person's research as one's own.

Purchasing or commissioning research papers.

Translating published work into another language without appropriate citation.

Reusing substantial portions of one's previously published work without disclosure (self-plagiarism).

.Similarity Screening

Every manuscript submitted to IJAQC undergoes plagiarism screening before entering the peer-review process using recognized plagiarism detection software such as Crossref Similarity Check (iThenticate) or equivalent similarity detection tools.

The similarity report is carefully evaluated by the Editorial Office to distinguish legitimate citations, references, standard technical terminology, and properly quoted material from unethical copying.

A high similarity percentage does not automatically indicate plagiarism; every report is evaluated within its academic context.

3. Acceptable Similarity Limits

Similarity Index Editorial Action
Up to 10% Generally acceptable, subject to editorial evaluation.
11%-20% Authors may be requested to revise the manuscript and reduce similarity before peer review.
Above 20% Manuscript may be rejected due to excessive similarity or suspected plagiarism.

Regardless of the overall similarity score, extensive copying from a single source, unattributed text, copied software code, datasets, algorithms, figures, or evidence of intentional plagiarism may result in immediate rejection.

The Editor-in-Chief reserves the right to make the final decision based on the nature, extent, and context of the overlap.

4. Self-Plagiarism

Authors must avoid self-plagiarism by ensuring that previously published material is not reproduced without:

Proper citation.

Appropriate disclosure.

Significant new scientific contribution.

Permission where required.

Simultaneous submission of substantially similar manuscripts to multiple journals or duplicate publication of the same research is considered unethical publishing practice.

5. Artificial Intelligence (AI)-Generated Content

The use of Artificial Intelligence tools for language editing, grammar correction, or manuscript preparation may be acceptable when used responsibly.

However:

AI systems or software cannot be listed as authors.

Authors remain fully responsible for the originality, accuracy, integrity, and scientific validity of the manuscript.

AI-generated text, images, code, datasets, references, or analyses must not be presented as original scholarly work without appropriate disclosure where required.

Authors must verify all AI-assisted outputs for accuracy and proper citation.

The use of AI must not result in plagiarism, fabricated references, misleading information, or unethical research practices.

6. Editorial Actions in Cases of Plagiarism

If plagiarism or publication misconduct is identified before publication, IJAQC may:

Reject the manuscript immediately.

Request revision and proper citation where the overlap is minor and unintentional.

Suspend the editorial review pending investigation.

Notify all authors regarding the findings.

If plagiarism is identified after publication, the journal may:

Publish a correction or clarification.

Retract the article.

Remove the article from active publication where legally required.

Notify the author's institution, employer, funding agency, or relevant academic authority.

Restrict future submissions from the responsible author(s) for an appropriate period.

7. Responsibilities of Authors

Authors are expected to:

Submit only original and unpublished work.

Properly acknowledge all sources of information.

Obtain permission before reproducing copyrighted material.

Ensure the authenticity of data, software, algorithms, and research findings.

Verify manuscript originality before submission.

Cooperate fully with any investigation concerning suspected plagiarism or publication misconduct.

Submission of a manuscript to IJAQC constitutes a declaration that the work is original and has not been copied from any published or unpublished source without proper acknowledgment.

8. Responsibilities of Reviewers and Editors

Reviewers and editors play an essential role in safeguarding publication ethics.

They are expected to:

Report suspected plagiarism or duplicate publication.

Maintain confidentiality throughout the investigation.

Evaluate similarity reports objectively.

Follow fair, transparent, and unbiased procedures while handling ethical concerns.

Recommend appropriate action whenever publication misconduct is suspected.

Editorial decisions shall always be based on evidence and internationally accepted ethical publishing standards.

9. Investigation Procedure

All allegations of plagiarism are investigated confidentially by the Editorial Office.

The investigation may include:

Examination of the similarity report.

Comparison with the original source(s).

Communication with the corresponding author.

Review by the Editor-in-Chief and Editorial Board, where appropriate.

Consultation with independent experts, if necessary.

Final editorial decision based on the available evidence.

Authors shall be given an opportunity to respond before any final decision is taken.

10. Consequences of Plagiarism

Depending on the seriousness of the misconduct, IJAQC may impose one or more of the following actions:

Manuscript rejection.

Mandatory revision.

Withdrawal before publication.

Publication of a correction or retraction.

Temporary or permanent restriction on future submissions.

Notification to the author's institution, employer, funding agency, or relevant academic authorities.

Any other action considered appropriate under internationally accepted publication ethics standards.

11. Commitment to Academic Integrity

The International Journal of Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing & Cyber Security (IJAQC) is dedicated to promoting originality, ethical scholarship, transparency, and responsible scientific publishing. The journal encourages authors, reviewers, editors, and readers to uphold the principles of honesty, accountability, fairness, and academic integrity in all aspects of scholarly communication.

By submitting a manuscript to IJAQC, authors acknowledge that they have read, understood, and agreed to comply with this Plagiarism Policy and accept full responsibility for the originality, authenticity, and ethical integrity of their research.
