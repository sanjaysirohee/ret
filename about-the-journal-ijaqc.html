ABOUT THE JOURNAL

International Journal of Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing & Cyber Security (IJAQC)

The International Journal of Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing & Cyber Security (IJAQC) is an international, peer-reviewed, open-access scholarly journal dedicated to publishing high-quality, original, and innovative research in the fields of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Quantum Computing, Cyber Security, Computer Science, Information Technology, Data Science, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Blockchain, Internet of Things (IoT), Cloud Computing, Robotics, Natural Language Processing (NLP), Computer Vision, and other emerging technologies.

IJAQC provides a global platform for researchers, academicians, scientists, engineers, industry professionals, policymakers, and research scholars to disseminate cutting-edge research, exchange innovative ideas, and promote interdisciplinary collaboration in rapidly evolving technological domains.

The journal publishes original research articles, review papers, systematic literature reviews, case studies, technical notes, conceptual papers, and short communications that significantly contribute to scientific knowledge, technological innovation, and practical applications. Every submitted manuscript undergoes a rigorous Double-Blind Peer Review process to ensure originality, scientific quality, technical excellence, and ethical integrity.

The journal encourages multidisciplinary and interdisciplinary research that bridges academia, industry, government, and society by addressing current challenges and future opportunities in intelligent computing, secure digital systems, and next-generation technologies.

Aim

The International Journal of Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing & Cyber Security (IJAQC) aims to:

Publish high-quality, original, and impactful research in Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing, Cyber Security, and emerging technologies.

Promote innovation and interdisciplinary scientific research.

Provide an international platform for researchers, academicians, industry experts, and policymakers.

Encourage collaboration between academia, industry, and government organizations.

Support the development of intelligent, secure, and sustainable technological solutions.

Advance scientific knowledge through ethical, transparent, and rigorous scholarly publishing

Peer Review Process

All submitted manuscripts undergo an initial editorial screening followed by a rigorous Double-Blind Peer Review by at least two independent experts. Editorial decisions are based solely on originality, scientific merit, methodological rigor, technical quality, ethical compliance, and relevance to the journal's scope.

Open Access Policy

IJAQCS follows the Gold Open Access publishing model. All accepted articles are published online with immediate and unrestricted access, allowing readers worldwide to read, download, share, and cite published research without subscription or access fees.

Publication Ethics

The journal adheres to internationally recognised publication ethics and follows the principles and best practices recommended by COPE. Authors, reviewers, editors, and the publisher are expected to maintain the highest standards of honesty, transparency, fairness, and academic integrity throughout the publication process.
